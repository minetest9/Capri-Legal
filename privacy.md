# Capri — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

Capri is a private, fan-made Discord bot that hangs out in **The Goofy Goobers** Discord server: she plays music in voice calls, chats in text and DMs, and talks out loud in voice channels. She is run by one person for a group of friends (the "operator", Discord user **PinkleTwin**). This page explains what she does with your information.

## What Capri receives

- **Voice in a call she has joined.** While Capri is in a voice channel, she receives each person's voice audio from Discord. The audio is turned into text by speech-recognition software running on Capri's own server. **Audio is never recorded or stored**; only the resulting text is kept (see below).
- **Messages.** Direct messages you send her, and messages in the channels of The Goofy Goobers that she can read (for example when you mention her, reply to her or say her name). When she answers, she also reads the last few messages of that conversation so her reply fits.
- **Pictures, GIFs, stickers and voice messages** you send her, so she can react to them.
- **Basic Discord information** such as your username, server nickname, who is in a voice channel and who is speaking.

## What Capri keeps

- **Short-term context:** the last few minutes of a call and recent chat, kept in memory so her replies make sense. This is not saved permanently.
- **Long-term memory notes:** short notes about things people say about themselves (for example "plays Deadlock" or "favorite song is …"), stored with their Discord name.
- **A diary:** short summaries of past calls.
- **Service logs** on her server, which can include the text of messages and transcribed speech. They are used only to keep her working and to fix problems.

Memory notes and the diary are kept until they are deleted. The operator can view and delete them at any time, and will do so on request.

## Who else processes your information

To reply, Capri sends the relevant text (your message or transcribed speech, recent context and her memory notes) to:

- **TabbyCluster's AI service** (ai.tabbycluster.net), which forwards it to third-party AI model providers (for example Groq, Google and MiniMax) to generate her reply. Pictures you send her may be described by an AI vision model the same way.
- **A web search service**, only when you ask her a factual question she needs to look up (for example the weather or the news). Only the search question is sent.
- **Spotify**, to play the music people request.
- **Discord**, which carries all messages and voice.

Capri does **not** sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

## Where it is stored

On a private server operated for Capri, located in the United States.

## Your choices and rights

- You can avoid Capri's text replies by not messaging, mentioning or naming her, and her voice processing by not being in a voice channel she is in.
- You can ask the operator to show you, correct or delete what Capri has stored about you (memory notes, diary entries and log lines).
- Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under data-protection law (for example the GDPR); the operator will honour them.

To ask, message **PinkleTwin** on Discord (in The Goofy Goobers server).

## Children

Capri is meant for members of The Goofy Goobers, who must meet Discord's minimum age. She is not directed at children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Capri changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
